# PLG Business Rules

Status: Active

## Pricing Revenue Streams

PLG recognizes three distinct revenue streams.

### Parts Margin

Purpose: Profit earned from buying and reselling parts.

- PLG may recommend the margin.
- The user retains final control.
- Current tiered markup rules remain active unless replaced by an approved pricing specification.
- Parts Margin must remain separate from Service Charges and Sourcing Fees in internal reporting.

### Sourcing Fee

Purpose: Compensation for sourcing expertise.

Examples include:

- locating difficult parts;
- contacting multiple suppliers;
- OEM research;
- cross-referencing;
- technical verification;
- coordinating multiple sources.

Rules:

- Optional.
- Stored on the Job.
- PLG may recommend an amount.
- The user can edit or remove it.
- It must be reported separately in Business Intelligence.

### Service Charge

Purpose: Compensation for additional work personally performed.

Examples include:

- equipment inspection;
- used-part inspection;
- delivery to a shipping agent or freight forwarder;
- customer or supplier visits;
- programming;
- measurements;
- technical consulting;
- special coordination;
- after-hours work.

Rules:

- Optional.
- Stored on the Job.
- PLG does not recommend the amount.
- If used, the minimum is $150.
- There is no maximum.
- The user may edit or remove it.
- PLG should remind the user before quote generation when appropriate.
- Once already added, PLG should not ask again.
- It must be reported separately in Business Intelligence.

## Quote Reminder Philosophy

Reminders should guide without unnecessarily blocking work.

Before generating a quote, PLG should act as a second set of eyes and surface possible missing revenue or workflow details.

The user must retain the ability to continue without adding an optional charge.

## Existing Pricing Tiers

Current parts markup rules:

- $0–$50 cost: 40%
- $50–$200 cost: 30%
- $200–$500 cost: 25%
- $500 and above: 20%

These remain unchanged until an approved Pricing Assistant specification replaces or extends them.
